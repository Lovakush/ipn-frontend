## Summary
# GuideEntrance.vue Summary

This Vue 3 component renders an entrance/landing section for a pet food guide quiz, with A/B testing functionality to conditionally display either a Jumbotron or Edito component variant. It accepts guide entrance parameters and a button flag as props, and includes hardcoded French copy and imagery promoting a "find your pet's ideal recipe" questionnaire that takes under 2 minutes to complete.
